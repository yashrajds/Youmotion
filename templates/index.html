YouMotion

Control YouTube videos with hand gestures

How to Use:

  • Paste a YouTube video URL above
  • Click "Start Controller" to begin
  • Use hand gestures to control playback:
    • Raise right hand → Play
    • Raise left hand → Pause
    • Swipe right → Fast-forward 10s
    • Swipe left → Rewind 10s
    • Push forward → Volume up
    • Pull back → Volume down
